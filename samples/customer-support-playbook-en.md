# Northstar Customer Support Playbook

This fictional document is included as an English retrieval sample.

## Response targets

Urgent cases involving account access or a complete service outage must receive
a first response within 20 minutes. High-priority cases have a two-hour target,
while normal questions should receive a first response within one business day.
The response target measures acknowledgement, not final resolution.

## Escalation

An agent should escalate a case when the customer reports a security incident,
when the same failure continues after two documented troubleshooting attempts,
or when a workaround would cause data loss. Security cases go directly to the
security on-call engineer. Billing disputes above 500 USD require approval from
the support lead.

Every escalation needs a concise summary, the observed behavior, steps already
attempted, relevant timestamps, and the customer's preferred contact channel.
Forwarding a long conversation without a summary is not considered a complete
handoff.

## Customer communication

Use direct language and state what is known, what remains unknown, and when the
next update will arrive. Do not promise a resolution time unless the responsible
engineering team has confirmed it. For an active outage, send an update at least
once every 45 minutes, even when there is no material change.

## Closing a case

Before closing, confirm that the customer can use the affected feature and add a
short internal note describing the actual fix. Cases closed automatically after
seven days of customer inactivity can be reopened for 30 days without creating
a new ticket.
